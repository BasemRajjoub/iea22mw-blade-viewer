What this is

An interactive 3D look at the blade of the IEA Wind 22 MW offshore reference wind turbine, using its real composite ply layup. Chord, twist, prebend, blended airfoils, 12 shell layers and the shear webs are all read straight from the turbine's WindIO YAML definition. It's meant for checking geometry, so ply drops show up as hard steps.

Reference turbine & data

Blade geometry and layup come from the IEA-22-280-RWT repository (IEA-22-280-RWT.yaml, in WindIO format), published by IEA Wind TCP with DTU Wind and NREL.

F. Zahle, A. Barlas, K. Lønbæk, P. Bortolotti, D. Zalkind, L. Wang, C. Labuschagne, L. Sethuraman, G. Barter. Definition of the IEA Wind 22-Megawatt Offshore Reference Wind Turbine. DTU Wind Energy Report E-0243, Technical University of Denmark, 2024. doi:10.11581/DTU.00000317
